The mission

An open platform where an idea is enough to start.

📞 Any channel

Send an idea by phone call, voice note or WhatsApp. No laptop, no forms, no fees. No money is asked for, and none is paid.

⚖️ Fair shares

Contribution points depend on the idea alone: no early bonus, no late penalty. A tamper-evident ledger keeps every allocation honest.

🌱 Enhance, don't duplicate

If an idea already exists in the market, the work goes into improving it, and the contributor still earns credit for the enhancement.
